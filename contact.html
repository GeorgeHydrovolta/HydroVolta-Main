We assess the fit.

Hydrovolta qualifies projects based on source water chemistry, target use, flow requirements, and site constraints. Our engineering team will assess whether SonixED™, a brine valorization pathway, or an integrated configuration is the right answer — and tell you honestly if it is not.

info@hydrovolta.com linkedin.com/company/hydrovolta

What to send

TDS, pH, hardness (Ca, Mg), nitrate, chloride, sulphate, silica, target flow, target use, discharge constraints. Brine composition for valorization enquiries.

What we assess

Technical fit, treatment route, pilot configuration, recovery targets, valorization feasibility, scale-up pathway, and relevant funding mechanisms for your geography.

What we avoid

We do not recommend SonixED™ when another approach is more appropriate. Every assessment begins with an honest GO / NO-GO determination.
