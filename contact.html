Engineering Assessment

Send the water data.
We assess the fit.

Hydrovolta qualifies projects based on source water chemistry, target use, flow requirements, and site constraints. Our engineering team will assess whether SonixED™ is the right answer — and tell you honestly if it is not.

What to send

TDS, pH, hardness (Ca, Mg), nitrate, chloride, sulphate, silica, target flow, target use, discharge constraints. Brine composition for valorisation enquiries.

What we assess

Technical fit, treatment route, pilot configuration, recovery targets, valorisation feasibility, scale-up pathway, and relevant funding mechanisms for your geography.

What we avoid

We do not recommend SonixED™ when another approach is more appropriate. Every assessment begins with an honest GO / NO-GO determination.
