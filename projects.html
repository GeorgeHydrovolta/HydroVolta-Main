Active deployment

Morocco — the integrated SonixED™ + brine valorization reference.

Morocco is Hydrovolta’s flagship active project. It is the first commercial demonstration of the integrated SonixED™ desalination and downstream brine valorization model — validating both capabilities in a real operating environment simultaneously.

Active Project · 2025–2028

Saline water desalination with integrated brine valorization

Seawater-intruded coastal aquifers at approximately 7,000 ppm TDS. SonixED™ delivers saline water desalination for irrigation and drinking water supply. Residual concentrated brine is processed through downstream valorization for chemical recovery. Performance tracked through site-specific operating data.

Feed source

Outputs

Drinking water and irrigation supply

Industrial reference

Germany — cooling tower blowdown, chemical industry.

Completed 90-day pilot at a chemical industry facility in Germany. Head-to-head comparison against RO and EDR on the same feed water. Published case study with operating data.

Completed Pilot · Feb–May 2023

High-hardness CTBD treatment — SonixED™ vs RO vs EDR

Cooling tower blowdown at a major chemical industry facility. Feed water: TDS 4,500 ppm, silica 85 mg/l, high hardness — exactly the conditions where conventional membranes fail. SonixED™ was run against RO and standard EDR on the same feed water over 90 days.

SonixED™ recovery

90–98%

vs RO 50–75%, EDR 70–85%

Energy consumption

0.19–0.7 kWh/m³

vs RO 2.0–4.0, EDR 1.0–2.0

Annual discharge reduction

43,680 m³/yr

vs RO 26,880, EDR 34,176

Total treatment cost

$0.30–0.40/m³

vs RO $0.60–1.00, EDR $0.40–0.80
