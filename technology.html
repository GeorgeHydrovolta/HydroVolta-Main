SonixED™ Technology

Three integrated mechanisms. One validated platform.

Each component addresses a specific failure mode of conventional membrane treatment in the 1,500–20,000 ppm TDS brackish range. Together they deliver recovery and scaling resistance that individual technologies cannot achieve independently.

01

Electrodialysis Reversal (EDR)

Electric potential drives dissolved ions through ion-exchange membranes. Unlike reverse osmosis, water does not pass through the membrane — ions do. Periodic polarity reversal cleans membranes in-situ without chemical intervention.

  • Stable output under variable TDS feed conditions
  • Polarity reversal eliminates chemical CIP dependency
  • Lower hydraulic pressure than RO — 0.5–2.5 bar vs 25–35 bar
02

Ultrasound Integration (Patent 2021)

Hydrovolta's granted patent integrates ultrasound transducers directly at the membrane surface. Acoustic cavitation continuously disrupts scale nucleation — addressing the primary failure mode that defeats conventional membrane systems on silica-laden sources.

  • Acoustic cavitation prevents calcium, magnesium, and silica scale
  • 50–90% reduction in CIP cycles in validated operating contexts
  • Extended membrane operational lifespan
03

Custom Membrane Coating

A proprietary membrane coating enables monovalent ion selectivity. The system removes nitrate, sodium, chloride, and fluoride while retaining beneficial calcium and magnesium. Output meets drinking water and agricultural standards without full demineralisation.

  • Selective nitrate removal — validated at Water Corporation Perth (3 utility sites)
  • Mineral-balanced output — no post-treatment remineralisation needed
  • Configurable per application ion targets

Brine Valorisation

Chemical recovery where the concentrate chemistry supports it

Where SonixED™ desalination produces residual brine with suitable ion composition, Hydrovolta evaluates and pilots chemical recovery using electromembrane separation and complementary technologies.

  • Brine composition assessed before any valorisation pilot is recommended
  • Target product streams: HCl, NaOH, Mg(OH)₂, Ca(OH)₂ depending on chemistry
  • Configuration not fixed — designed per brine type and project objectives
  • Active alongside SonixED™ desalination in the Morocco integrated deployment
