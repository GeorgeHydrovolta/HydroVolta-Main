Deployment pathway

Validate before you scale. Always.

The SalinBloc™ pilot is not a product to sell — it is a risk-reduction instrument for the buyer. It generates a site-specific performance dataset that no specification sheet can replace. Scale-up is engineered from that data.

01

Water analysis

Source chemistry reviewed. Technical fit assessed. GO / NO-GO determined before hardware is specified.

02

Pilot design

SalinBloc™ configured for your source. CAPEX/OPEX benchmarked. Funding pathway identified.

03

On-site pilot

SalinBloc™ commissioned with full monitoring. Real operating data generated. Brine valorization potential evaluated in parallel.

04

Scale

Full installation engineered from pilot data. Parallel SalinBloc™ units or engineered skid — only when data supports it.
