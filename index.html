The yield gap

Water you already own — that your system can't reach

Every water treatment project starts with a source. Conventional systems recover 65–75% of it. The rest becomes brine — water you paid to extract, that you cannot use, and must manage as waste.

  • In a 1,000 m³/day operation, that's 250–350 m³ of discarded water, every day.
  • The infrastructure — well, pump, pre-treatment — costs the same whether you recover 70% or 90%.
  • Every additional m³ you recover lowers your cost per m³ delivered against fixed capital already committed.
  • As aquifer TDS increases over time, conventional recovery rates fall further. SonixED™ was designed for this trajectory.
Why operators face this every day

Four reasons your current system leaves water behind

Conventional treatment technology was designed for cleaner source water. As your source becomes more challenging, these problems compound.

You are pumping water you cannot fully use

Conventional systems recover 65–75% of source water. The remaining 25–35% becomes reject brine — water your infrastructure already paid to extract, that you cannot deliver. In water-scarce regions, that gap is not a technical detail. It is a supply shortfall.

Scaling forces you to run conservative

To protect membranes and surfaces from silica and hardness scaling, operators lower recovery rates. The system designed to deliver water becomes the reason you cannot push harder. Maintenance shutdowns compound the daily loss — and chemical cleaning cycles are expensive and frequent.

Your source water is getting harder every year

Aquifer draw-down, seawater intrusion, and seasonal variation all increase salinity over time. A system performing at 72% recovery today may reach 62% within three years. Conventional performance degrades as conditions worsen. SonixED™ was designed for exactly this trajectory.

Full capital commitment before proven performance

Sizing and funding a complete installation without demonstrated recovery data on your specific source water is a significant risk — for your organisation and for whoever is financing the project. Specifications from a reference case on different water are not evidence.

SonixED™ Technology

How 90%+ recovery is achieved — and sustained

SonixED™ combines Electrodialysis Reversal with integrated ultrasound. Each element solves a specific barrier to high recovery on difficult source water.

90%+

High-Recovery Ion Separation

Electrodialysis Reversal selectively removes dissolved ions — nitrate, sodium, chloride, fluoride — while retaining beneficial minerals including calcium and magnesium. Recovery rates of 90% or above are achievable in suitable applications, compared to 65–75% for conventional membrane treatment.

Validated performance
50–90%

Ultrasound Anti-Scaling

Integrated ultrasound disrupts scale formation on stack surfaces in real time — silica, hardness, and mineral deposits that force conventional systems to run conservatively. This is what allows SonixED™ to maintain high recovery rates without the chemical anti-scalant cycles that drive operational cost.

50–90% reduction in CIP chemical cycles — validated applications
<5%

Minimal Residual Brine

Because recovery rates are high, the residual brine volume is small — less than 5–10% of intake in suitable applications. Where brine valorisation is relevant — acid and base recovery, selective mineral extraction — the concentrated stream can be processed further rather than disposed of.
