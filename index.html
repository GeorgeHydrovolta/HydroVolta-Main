What we do

Groundwater desalination is the core. Brine valorization is the extension.

Hydrovolta’s commercial model is clear: we treat difficult groundwater and recover more usable water from it. Where the residual brine has the right chemistry and economics, we extend into selective valorization. Both capabilities are active in the same deployment in Morocco.

Core business

High-recovery groundwater desalination

Brackish wells, hard groundwater, saline intrusion, nitrate and salinity challenges. SonixED™ recovers up to 98% of feed water in suitable applications — with selective ion removal, significantly lower residual brine, and reduced chemical dependency versus conventional membrane treatment.

Technology

SonixED™ — electrodialysis reversal strengthened by ultrasound.

SonixED™ combines electrodialysis reversal, ultrasound-assisted scaling control, and a proprietary selective membrane coating. Developed through institutional research partnerships over eight years. Patent granted 2021. Projects, pilots and pipeline across four continents.

EDR

Electrodialysis Reversal

Electric potential drives selected ions through membranes. Polarity reversal cleans membranes without chemical intervention. Stable output under variable feed TDS.

US

Ultrasound Anti-Scaling

Acoustic cavitation at the membrane surface prevents silica and hard-water scaling. 50–90% reduction in CIP cycles in validated operating contexts. Extended membrane lifespan.

SEL

Selective Ion Removal

Custom membrane coating removes nitrate, sodium, chloride, fluoride while retaining calcium and magnesium. Mineral-balanced output. No post-treatment remineralisation required.

Reference projects

Active deployments, utility pilots, and development pipeline.

Projects are labeled accurately by maturity. Hydrovolta has active equipment generating operational data, a contracted utility pilot, and signed LOI and MOU-stage pipeline across four continents.

Active Project

Morocco — Saline Water Desalination with Brine Valorization

Seawater-intruded coastal aquifer at approximately 7,000 ppm TDS. SonixED™ delivering saline water desalination with integrated downstream brine valorization. Funded by the Flanders Climate Action Program. Hydrovolta’s primary integrated reference site.

Utility Pilot Reference

Perth, Australia — Water Corporation Perth

Selective nitrate and salinity removal across three public utility sites — supplying clean water for drinking, irrigation, agriculture, and livestock. Cloud telemetry and regulatory monitoring active. Hydrovolta’s primary utility reference internationally.

LOI Signed

Brazil — Remote Groundwater Remediation

Development-stage project for nitrate and salinity co-contamination at a remote site. High-recovery treatment and minimal brine disposal are central to the technical and economic case.

Development-stage opportunity for selective ion recovery from deep brackish groundwater and brine streams. Pilot design in progress. MOU signed.
