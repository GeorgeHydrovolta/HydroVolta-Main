Privacy Policy

This policy covers how Hydrovolta NV handles personal data submitted through engineering assessment and contact forms on this website.

Data submitted through assessment forms

Project data, contact details, water chemistry data, and any analysis files submitted through the engineering assessment form or the full engineering questionnaire are handled as confidential business information.

This data is used solely to evaluate technical fit and respond to the submitted enquiry. It is not shared with third parties except where required for project assessment with approved Hydrovolta research or engineering partners, and only where necessary for that specific assessment.

Contact and communication data

Contact details provided through this website — including name, email address, telephone number, and organisation — are used to respond to enquiries and, where relevant, to provide project updates or follow-up technical information.

Contact data is not used for unsolicited marketing and is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

Website analytics

This website may use analytics tools to understand visitor behaviour in aggregate. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. Where such tools are in use, they are configured to anonymise IP addresses.

Embedded video content

This website includes embedded YouTube video players (youtube-nocookie.com). Videos are loaded only when you click to play them, using the privacy-enhanced embed mode, which limits the data YouTube collects before interaction. If you choose to play an embedded video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to data collected during that session. Hydrovolta does not receive or store data from video playback.

Form submission processing

Assessment and contact forms on this website are submitted via FormSubmit.co, a third-party form routing service. FormSubmit forwards submitted form data to Hydrovolta's email inbox and does not retain form data beyond the delivery transaction. By submitting a form on this site, you acknowledge that form data passes through this service in transit.

Data retention and your rights

Personal data submitted through this website is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose it was submitted — typically the duration of technical evaluation and any resulting project correspondence.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data Hydrovolta holds about you. To exercise these rights, contact george@hydrovolta.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Hydrovolta NV is registered in Belgium (BE 0664.974.129) and processes data in accordance with applicable Belgian and EU data protection law (GDPR).
