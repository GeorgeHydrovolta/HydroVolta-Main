Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers how Hydrovolta BV handles personal data submitted through engineering assessment and contact forms on this website.

Data submitted through assessment forms

Project data, contact details, water chemistry data, and uploaded analysis files submitted through the engineering assessment form are handled as confidential business information. This data is used solely for the purpose of evaluating technical fit and responding to the submitted enquiry. It is not shared with third parties except where required for project assessment with approved Hydrovolta research or engineering partners.

Contact and communication data

Contact details provided through this website are used to respond to enquiries and, where relevant, to provide project updates. Contact data is not used for unsolicited marketing.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies until you choose to allow them. Necessary cookies, which the site needs to function, are always active. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie banner.

The services we use, and what each one is for:

  • Google Analytics 4 (analytics) — aggregate measurement of how the site is used. Loaded through Google Tag Manager, and only after you allow analytics cookies.
  • Google Ads (marketing) — measures whether an advertisement led to an enquiry. Loaded only after you allow marketing cookies.
  • Google Tag Manager (necessary) — the container that loads the above according to your choice. It sets no cookies of its own.
  • Zoho (necessary) — our customer relationship system and the service that delivers enquiries to us. What you submit through a form on this site is posted to hydrovolta.com, recorded as an enquiry in Zoho CRM and emailed to our team; it is not stored by a third-party form service. Zoho processes this data on our instructions under a data processing agreement.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag (marketing) — measures whether a LinkedIn advertisement led to an enquiry, and allows us to show advertisements to similar audiences. Nothing is loaded from LinkedIn until you allow marketing cookies.
  • Meta Pixel (marketing) — measures whether a Facebook or Instagram advertisement led to an enquiry. Nothing is loaded from Meta until you allow marketing cookies.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (necessary) — checks that a form was submitted by a person rather than a bot. It sets no advertising cookies and does not track you across sites.
  • YouTube (video) — embedded video, served in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing loads from YouTube until you allow video cookies.

Your cookie choice is stored in your browser under hv_consent. It records which categories you allowed and when. It is not shared with anyone and is not used to identify you.

If you reach this site from an advertisement, the campaign parameters in the link (and the Google click identifier) are held for the duration of your visit and submitted with the form, so we know which campaign an enquiry came from.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some of the services above are provided by companies established outside the European Economic Area, or store data outside it: Google (analytics and advertising), LinkedIn, Meta, and Zoho, whose account for this website is hosted in the United States. Where personal data reaches these providers, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, and for providers certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework by that framework. We keep the data we send to them to the minimum the purpose requires: the advertising services receive the fact that an enquiry happened and the campaign it came from, not the contents of your water analysis.

How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not become projects — deleted 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Newsletter — kept until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any message we send.
  • Customers — kept for the duration of the contract and then for as long as Belgian law requires us to retain commercial and accounting records.

Email from us

We answer enquiries at the address you give us. We send news about Hydrovolta only to people who have ticked the box asking for it, and we record when that box was ticked. You can unsubscribe from any message, and doing so does not affect an enquiry you have already sent us.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to info@hydrovolta.com. If you believe we have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels.

This policy was last reviewed 4 November 2026. Data controller: Hydrovolta BV, Leuven, Belgium — company registration BE 0652.996.179.
