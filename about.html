Company History

From scientific hypothesis to deployed technology

George Brik and Yousef Yousef founded Hydrovolta NV in May 2016 in Leuven, Belgium. George brings over 25 years of industrial water engineering experience across Europe and the Middle East, specialising in advanced membrane architectures, electrodialysis systems, and high-purity water treatment.

SonixED™ was developed through a sustained research collaboration with VITO (the Flemish Institute for Technological Research) and a broader network of high-level institutional research partners including CNR and others. Following eight years of rigorous laboratory validation, the foundational process patent was granted in 2021.

Supported by a €2.2M deep-tech development grant from the European Innovation Council (EIC) Accelerator, Hydrovolta now operates active projects in Morocco and Australia, with pipeline development in Brazil and Saudi Arabia. Brine valorisation is confirmed as a core element of the R&D portfolio, with the Morocco deployment combining SonixED™ desalination and downstream brine valorisation in a single integrated project.
